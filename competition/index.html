SSBC 2027

Sydney Schools Business Competition 2027

You and your team advise Southern Cross Technologies, a fictional Sydney company stuck with eight million unsold tags. Over five weeks, new information arrives in stages. Each stage, each mile, asks for a new decision and a new deliverable.

Register your team Read the rules

The route

Five weeks, from Mile 0 to the Live Final.

Exact opening and closing times are emailed to every registered team when each stage opens. Late work isn't marked.

  1. MILE0

    1–2 FebAll teams

    Mile 0: Briefing Check

    A short check that your team has read the Opening Case. Five or six auto-marked questions, ten minutes once opened. Not scored: it just makes sure nobody starts behind.

    Submit One set of answers per team

  2. MILE1

    3–10 FebAll teams

    Mile 1: Where to Play

    Market data, customer interviews and a survey. Decide which markets to enter first, which to leave for later, and which to walk away from.

    Submit Decision worksheet · 150–250 word memo · One evidence calculation

  3. MILE2

    11–17 FebAll teams

    Mile 2: Making It Pay

    Choose how the company makes money, build a three-year plan inside the budget, and test what happens if sales come in slower than hoped.

    Submit Three-year and investment plan · 150–250 word memo · 2–3 minute recorded pitch (every member speaks)

  4. GATE✓

    21 FebSchools

    School nominations

    Each school nominates one team to continue, based on Mile 1 and Mile 2.

  5. MILE3

    22–28 FebNominated teams

    Mile 3: Project Kestrel

    Something unexpected lands on the CEO's desk. Decide whether to accept, reject or negotiate, and show what changes in your plan.

    Submit Response memo (up to 400 words) · “What changes” table · One-page term sheet

  6. FINAL★

    4 MarTop 10 teams

    Live Final at Sydney Grammar School

    Present your strategy in up to seven minutes, take five minutes of judges' questions, then respond live to new information you haven't seen before.

The top ten teams overall, ranked on Mile 1, Mile 2 and Project Kestrel, are invited to the Live Final at Sydney Grammar School on 4 March 2027.

Who can enter

Any school. Any background.

  • Students at any NSW secondary school, including Years 9 and 10
  • Teams of three to four students from the same school
  • Up to three teams per school; each student in one team only
  • One staff member at your school who knows your team is taking part
  • Free to enter. No paid software or databases needed.
  • Register by 2 February 2027

Fair play

Your thinking, declared and defended.

  • Use AI for research and editing, then declare it in a short note with every submission.
  • The analysis and decisions must be your team's own. Any member may be asked to explain any number.
  • Use the case figures, plus up to two named outside sources across the whole competition.
  • Teachers and parents can teach general skills, but must not write or shape your analysis.
  • Don't share the case materials publicly before the Live Final.

Full rules

How you're judged

Five criteria, every stage.

The weights shift to match what each stage tests. Submissions are coded so markers don't see school names, teams near the finalist cut-off are marked twice, and sponsors have no say in results.

Problem framing
Did you find the central problem and build a clear strategy around it?
Evidence and analysis
Is the evidence relevant, read correctly and used to support your recommendation?
Feasibility and numbers
Could the company actually do this, and do the numbers add up?
Recommendation and trade-offs
Did you make a clear call and admit the main risks and alternatives?
Communication
Is it clear, concise and persuasive?
Weighting of each criterion by stage. Mile 0 is auto-marked and not weighted.

If something goes wrong

  • Technical problem? Contact us before the deadline, not after.
  • Need a reasonable adjustment, such as extra time? Ask before the stage opens.
  • Appeals are in writing, within three days of results, and only for procedural errors.

Questions

Before you sign up.

Do we need to know finance?

No. Every term is explained the first time it appears, and the case gives you every number you need. Judges reward clear thinking over jargon.

How much time does it take?

Roughly a few hours per mile, spread across a week, mostly outside class time. Mile 0 takes ten minutes.

Can Year 9 and 10 students enter?

Yes. Any student at a NSW secondary school can enter.

Our school doesn't have a business society. Can we still enter?

Yes. You only need a staff member who knows you're taking part. If you'd like to start a society too, the Starter Toolkit shows you how.

Can we use AI tools?

For research and editing, yes, as long as you declare it in a short note with each submission. The analysis and decisions must be your team's own.

What if our team isn't nominated?

You still get feedback on your Mile 1 and Mile 2 work, and you've completed a real case from start to finish.

Where can we read the case?

The Opening Case goes to registered teams before Mile 0. Case materials aren't published on the open web before the Live Final.
