Register your team

From zero to a registered team.

Everything a student needs to start a business society, run a first meeting and get a team ready for SSBC. You need three or four interested students, one staff member and about four weeks. No finance background required.

Four weeks, seven steps

  1. Find two or three peopleWeek 1. A small group that turns up beats a big list of names.
  2. Find a supervising staff memberWeek 1. Send them the teacher one-pager.
  3. Get permission to meetWeek 2. Keep the request small: one room, once a week.
  4. Run your first meetingWeek 3. Use the first meeting pack.
  5. Form teams of three or fourWeeks 3 to 4.
  6. Register your teamBy 2 February 2027.
  7. Practise before Mile 1Mile 1 opens 3 February 2027.
