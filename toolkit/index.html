Mile Zero Starter Toolkit · v1

From zero to a registered team.

Everything a student needs to start a business society, run a first meeting and get a team ready for SSBC. You don't need a finance background, a big group or a teacher who knows business. Just three or four interested students, one staff member and about four weeks.

Coming in January 2027

Practise before Mile 1.

Run one practice session a week in the four weeks before 3 February 2027. Request the toolkit and we'll email you when these are ready.

  • Three practice sessions: reading a case; will it pay for itself?; a three-year plan on one page
  • Two practice cases
  • The SSBC rubric with an annotated sample answer
  • A memo template with a worked example

For teachers

Small ask. Free. Student-run.

  • Required: know that a team from your school is taking part, and be the staff contact on their registration.
  • Optional: supervise a weekly lunchtime meeting if students start a society.
  • Not required: marking, teaching finance, travelling, or helping with the analysis.
