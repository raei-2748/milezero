Sydney Schools Business Competition · 2027

Every business mind starts at mile zero.

Mile Zero runs the Sydney Schools Business Competition: a free, staged business case competition for students from any NSW secondary school. No finance background needed. Just clear thinking, good evidence and the nerve to defend your decision.

The 2027 case

Eight million unsold tags. Losing value every month.

It's December 2026. A Sydney company is sitting on a warehouse of stock nobody ordered, and the board has one question: what now? Sell cheap, build something new, or turn a problem into a real business? Your team decides.

See the route

“We didn't acquire eight million microchips. We acquired eight million decisions about where this company goes next.”

Amelia Chen, CEO, Southern Cross Technologies (a fictional company)

The route

Five weeks. Four miles. One final.

New information arrives in stages. Each stage, each mile, asks for a new decision and a new deliverable. The top ten teams present live at Sydney Grammar School.
