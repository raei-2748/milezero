Pick a price to see what happens. Try them all.

The $300 for cups was spent before the fete opened, whatever price you pick. That's a sunk cost: it can't change, so it shouldn't change your decision. The best price is the one that brings in the most money from here, which is $4.

Now a rival stall opens next door selling a similar drink for $4. Do you match them, or drop to $3? There's no single right answer. A good answer makes a call, uses the numbers, and admits what could go wrong. That's exactly what {{brand.competitionShort}} asks, with a real-feeling company and much bigger numbers.
