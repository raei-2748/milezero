Who can enter

  • Students at any NSW secondary school, including Years 9 and 10
  • Teams of three to four students from the same school
  • Up to three teams per school, and each student in one team only
  • One staff member at your school who knows your team is taking part
  • Free to enter, with no paid software or databases needed
  • Register by {{dates.registrationCloses}}

How you're judged

The same five criteria at every stage, with weights that shift to match what each stage tests. Submissions are coded so markers don't see school names, teams near the finalist cut-off are marked twice, and sponsors have no say in results.

Questions

Do we need to know finance?

No. Every term is explained the first time it appears, and the case gives you every number you need. Judges reward clear thinking over jargon.

How much time does it take?

Roughly a few hours per mile, spread across a week, mostly outside class time. Mile 0 takes ten minutes.

Can Year 9 and 10 students enter?

Yes. Any student at a NSW secondary school can enter.

Our school doesn't have a business society. Can we still enter?

Yes. You only need a staff member who knows you're taking part. If you'd like to start a society too, the Starter Toolkit shows you how.

Can we use AI tools?

For research and editing, yes, as long as you declare it in a short note with each submission. The analysis and decisions must be your team's own.

What if our team isn't nominated?

You still get feedback on your Mile 1 and Mile 2 work, and you've completed a real case from start to finish.

Where can we read the case?

The Opening Case goes to registered teams before Mile 0. Case materials aren't published on the open web before the Live Final.
