Words you'll meet

Every term is also explained in the case the first time it appears.

Revenue
The money a business brings in from sales, before any costs come out.
Cost
What the business spends to make, buy or deliver what it sells.
Profit
Revenue minus costs. When it's negative, it's a loss.
Margin
Profit as a share of revenue. A 20% margin means keeping 20 cents of every dollar of sales.
Sunk cost
Money already spent that you can't get back, so it shouldn't drive your next decision. Try it in the mini case.
Payback period
How long an investment takes to earn back what it cost.
Market size
How much customers in a market spend, or could spend, in a year.
Trade-off
What you give up by choosing one option over another. Judges want you to name it.

How much does it cost to enter?

Nothing. Entry is free, and you don't need any paid software or databases.

How do we register?

Form a team of three or four students from your school, find one staff member who knows you're taking part, and register on the Get involved page before {{dates.registrationCloses}}. Each school can enter up to three teams.

Do we need to know finance?

No. Every term is explained the first time it appears, and the case gives you every number you need. Judges reward clear thinking over jargon.

How much time does it take?

Roughly a few hours per mile, spread across a week, mostly outside class time. Mile 0 takes ten minutes.

Can Year 9 and 10 students enter?

Yes. Any student at a NSW secondary school can enter.

Our school doesn't have a business society. Can we still enter?

Yes. You only need a staff member who knows you're taking part. If you'd like to start a society too, the Starter Toolkit shows you how.

Can we use AI tools?

For research and editing, yes, as long as you declare it in a short note with each submission. The analysis and decisions must be your team's own.

What if our team isn't nominated?

You still get feedback on your Mile 1 and Mile 2 work, and you've completed a real case from start to finish.

Where can we read the case?

The Opening Case goes to registered teams before Mile 0. Case materials aren't published on the open web before the Live Final.
